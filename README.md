## Tools with BAAs

Business associate agreements (BAAs) let vendors handle protected health information. These tools publish or document BAA availability.

- [GoHighLevel](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48000983084-hipaa-compliance-with-highlevel) - All-in-one CRM and marketing platform with a documented HIPAA compliance and BAA path.
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/legal/hipaa) - AI voice and speech platform with a documented HIPAA posture for Eleven Agents.
- [Telnyx](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/3347891-hipaa-baas-and-the-conduit-exception) - Communications and telephony API platform covering HIPAA, BAAs, and the conduit exception.
- [Drata](https://drata.com/products/compliance-automation) - Continuous compliance automation platform that can drive SOC 2 and HIPAA readiness.
- [Canary Mail](https://canarymail.io/faq) - Privacy-focused email client with secure-send options and a documented compliance story.

## Compliance Automation

- [Drata](https://drata.com/) - Continuous SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001, and GDPR compliance automation with evidence collection.
- [Certra](https://linkedin.com/company/certra-soc-hipaa) - Compliance advisory focused on SOC 2 and HIPAA readiness.
