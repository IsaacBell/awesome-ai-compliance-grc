## Compliance Automation

- [Vanta](https://www.vanta.com) - Automated security and compliance platform covering SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and GDPR.
- [Drata](https://drata.com/) - Continuous SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001, and GDPR compliance automation with evidence collection.
- [Secureframe](https://secureframe.com) - Compliance automation platform for SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and more.
- [Thoropass](https://www.thoropass.com) - Compliance automation platform with in-house auditors for SOC 2 and ISO 27001.
- [Sprinto](https://sprinto.com) - Automated compliance platform that continuously audits security controls.
- [Anecdotes](https://www.anecdotes.ai) - AI-powered GRC platform automating risk, compliance, and audit workflows.
- [TrustCloud](https://trustcloud.ai) - GRC platform that automates evidence collection and trust-center publishing.
- [Scrut](https://scrut.io) - Compliance automation with continuous control monitoring and audit management.
- [Certra](https://linkedin.com/company/certra-soc-hipaa) - Compliance advisory focused on SOC 2 and HIPAA readiness.
- [Hyperproof](https://hyperproof.io) - GRC platform for managing compliance operations and audit evidence.
- [AuditBoard](https://www.auditboard.com) - Cloud platform for audit, risk, and compliance teams.
- [LogicGate](https://www.logicgate.com) - Risk and compliance automation platform with workflow design.

## Privacy & Data Protection

- [TrustArc](https://trustarc.com) - Privacy compliance platform with assessments and consent management.
- [Ethyca](https://www.ethyca.com) - Privacy engineering infrastructure for automating data subject requests.
- [Transcend](https://transcend.io) - Data privacy infrastructure for consent and privacy request automation.

## AI Safety & Audit

- [Anthropic Responsible Scaling Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/responsible-scaling-policy) - Anthropic's framework for keeping frontier AI development within defined safety thresholds.
- [Anthropic AI Safety Levels](https://www.anthropic.com/news/ai-safety-levels) - Anthropic's system for classifying AI capability levels and the safeguards each requires.
- [OpenAI Frontier Risk & Preparedness Framework](https://openai.com/index/frontier-risk-and-preparedness/) - OpenAI's framework for identifying and mitigating frontier model risks.
- [Google Frontier Safety Framework](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/introducing-the-frontier-safety-framework/) - Google DeepMind's framework for mitigating severe AI risks from critical capabilities.
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) - US federal framework for managing AI-related risks.
- [OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-10-for-large-language-model-applications/) - The ten most critical vulnerabilities in LLM applications.
- [AI Incident Database](https://incidentdatabase.ai) - Public database of real-world AI incidents for risk analysis.

## GRC Frameworks & Guides

- [OCEG GRC Standards](https://www.oceg.org/grc-standards/) - The GRC Capability Model and related standards for governance, risk, and compliance programs.
- [IIA GRC Toolkits](https://www.iia.org.au/member-resources/governance-risk-and-compliance-grc-toolkits) - Practical toolkits from the Institute of Internal Auditors.
- [Diligent GRC Guide](https://www.diligent.com/resources/guides/grc) - Guide explaining governance, risk, and compliance for modern organizations.
- [COSO ERM Framework](https://www.coso.org/erm) - Widely adopted framework for enterprise risk management.
- [ISO 37301](https://www.iso.org/standard/75080.html) - International standard for compliance management systems.
- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) - US framework for managing cybersecurity risk across sectors.

- [US Executive Order on AI](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefing-room/presidential-actions/2023/10/30/executive-order-on-the-safe-secure-and-trustworthy-development-and-use-of-artificial-intelligence/) - Executive Order 14110 on safe, secure, and trustworthy AI.
